Why it's different

Same block as the other apps. Everything after it is ours.

Blockers stop the scroll and leave the time empty, so people go right back. BrainDiet fills it. It learns the goals you want and the person you want to be, then breaks them into steps small enough to do today. Everything stays on your phone.

Who's building it

Built by a student who had the problem.

I'm Jack Boring, a student at Arizona State. I'd pick up my phone and lose myself, and watch my days get wasted. Everyone around me was dealing with the same thing, and it was normal. So I researched what actually works, found what works for me, and built BrainDiet so it works for everyone.
