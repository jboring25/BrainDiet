## What this app IS
The first **nutrition tracker for the mind** — NOT a screen-time/productivity/parental-control app. Your mind becomes what you feed it. Every competitor sells *less* (block/limit/restrict); Brain Diet sells *more* — reclaim doomscroll time and pour it into who you want to become. **It redirects attention, it doesn't track it.**

- **The emotional north star = AGENCY.** User closes the app thinking *"I can actually do this."* Every color/type/copy/feature decision serves that.
- **You're selling a better IDENTITY, not less scrolling.** Never make the user feel they're quitting something — always that they're *becoming someone*. Celebrate what replaced the junk (books read, miles run), never what was avoided.
- **Success metric to optimize for:** *"I spent less time on my phone and more time becoming the person I want to be."*

## 🍽 PLATE COMPOSITION — three layers, effects past three (Jack, 2026-08-14)

A meal is **three components**, one per goal domain, composited as separate
transparent layers over a shared plate. Fixed z-order: main item at the back,
second in the middle, third in front. Every item carries its **own contact
shadow** as its own layer — that is what stops a cut-out from floating.

**Three is the minimum complete meal, not the maximum day.** Completions beyond
the third are rewarded with **light and motion on the existing plate**, never
with a fourth food render. The plate never overflows.

Generation: render the COMPLETE plated dish once, then cut the three components
and their contact shadows out of that single image. Components cut from one
render share lighting, scale and perspective automatically; three separately
generated items look like a collage. Cost is 6 files per dish plus 2 shared plate
files, and the 8 reachable states are composited at runtime, never rendered.

Nutrition vocabulary (protein / veg / carb) is an **internal asset naming
convention only** and must never appear as a UI label — see the metaphor rule
below.

**Three is a FLOOR, not a ceiling** (corrected 2026-08-15). Users are meant to
have more goals than the meal holds; the plate shows the first three and every
completion after that is rewarded without new art. So the domains step must
require **at least** 3 — never cap at 3. `selectedDomains` is unbounded in both
directions as of 2026-08-15, and the real hole is the floor: a user who picks two
domains can never complete a plate. Still open.

**The tiered win** (`PlateWin`, HomeHeroView): servings 1–2 fire sound + bounce
only; the third fires the full signature — drop → 360° toss → snap → gold flare,
with the steam BURSTING; every serving after fires ONE warm glint that sweeps the
plate and is gone. The beats are ranked against the meal, never against the count,
and the count itself belongs in Becoming. Effects mark **moments** and decay back
to calm — they must never accumulate into standing state, because a permanently
sparkling plate is the app competing to be the more interesting feed, which the
boredom section forbids.

## 🍽 HERO & PEDESTAL — backgrounds are always disconnected (Jack, 2026-08-14)

**Every background asset is generated as the environment ONLY.** No table, no
plate, no food, no dishes, no glasses, no cutlery — even when the reference photo
Jack supplies clearly contains them (his vineyard reference did; the table was
removed on the way in). The plate and the table are their own separate asset.

This is the Opal gem-on-a-pedestal arrangement: the plate is the hero object, the
table is what it rests on, and the background sits behind both and only adds
ambiance. It exists so the plate can move independently — it carries the
rubber-band stretch on overscroll and it changes as the user fills it, and none of
that is possible if it is welded into a photograph. It also keeps attention ranked:
the background must never pull focus from the plate.

`Asset Studio.command`) and land as markdown in `design/asset-requests/`.

## ⛔️ ANIMATION GUARDRAIL (Jack, 2026-08-06)

**Do not hand-build fluid animation in this app.** No SwiftUI motion systems, no
SpriteKit scenes, no hand-rolled physics for hero/ambient motion. Two passes on
the welcome hero were built this way and both were rejected — a PNG sliding and
bouncing across the screen is poor output no matter how correct the simulation
under it is. Fluid/hero animation is GENERATED (Kling, via the Higgsfield MCP);
the budget for it is approved.

Ordinary UI motion is unaffected: transitions, state changes, progress,
haptic-paired micro-interactions.

**The plate's steam is the worked example (2026-08-20).** It spent a month as
hand-drawn `Canvas` capsules with a white→gold gradient and shipped as a brown
smudge over the food. The rule caught it late because "four blurred rounded
rects" did not feel like animation. It is now filmed vapour — Kling footage,
composited and looped in `design/steam/`, shipped as HEVC-with-alpha. If you
find yourself writing `sin(t * frequency + phase)` to make something look
physical, that IS fluid animation and this rule applies to it.

## 🍽 THE METAPHOR RULE (Jack, 2026-08-09)

**Only use food language when it ADDS. Never overdone.**

THE TEST — remove the food word:
- Sentence means the same → it was **costume**. Cut it.
- Meaning gets **less clear** → the metaphor is TAKING AWAY. Cut it immediately;
  this is the one unforgivable case.
- Something is lost → it earns its place, by one of two routes:
  - **INSIGHT** — the food word explains what the plain word can't
    ("empty calories" says volume-without-value; "gray mush" says formless;
    "sugar, no meal" says instant hit, no substance)
  - **SURPRISE** — it lands as a fresh framing worth the space
    ("Your brain eats too." · "before the kitchen closes")

VOCABULARY (calibrated with Jack, 2026-08-09 — he knows what his generation
actually says; do not re-litigate from first principles):
- IN: slop · snack · fast food · grazing · doomscrolling · bloated · foggy ·
  craving · macros · balanced · protein · fiber · clean · nutrient-dense ·
  deficit · surplus · meal prep · nutrition label · tracking · logging ·
  fasting · servings · portions · plates · bites · dopamine ("frying your
  dopamine receptors" — their phrase for the harm, and already cooking language)
- OUT: **calories as a UNIT** (no honest conversion from screen time exists, so
  any such number is invented — see the honesty law). "Empty calories" survives
  as an IDIOM because it describes rather than measures.
  Also out: brain rot (dated, breaks the food motif) · ultra-processed (nerdy) ·
  sugar crash (nobody says it about phones)
- MARGINAL: cheat meal · munchies

ONE OBJECT, VERBALLY TOO. The plate is the app's single living object — don't
introduce rival nouns (a "fresh tray" for a fresh plate). That's the
one-living-object law applied to copy.

THE FOOD WORD MUST NOT CONTRADICT WHAT IT LABELS (added 2026-08-11). A "feast"
means abundance and indulgence; it labelled the 50-minute focus block, the
hardest thing on the menu. The word fought the thing. Now "Main courses."

NO ALLOWANCE FRAMING FOR SLOP (added 2026-08-11). "Budget," "allowance," and any
remainder ("40m left") turn slop into something you are entitled to spend and a
heavy day into an overdraft. Slop is OBSERVED and reported as a plain number,
never permitted in advance. (This is why `PlateEngine.servingsLeft` returns nil
for Dessert and Slop, and why the tray now reads "2h 10m of slop today.")

## 🥱 BOREDOM IS THE ARGUMENT (Jack, 2026-08-11)

**"It is so much healthier to be bored and not rely on constant dopamine. They
will get so much more work done."** This is a product position, not a copy note.

The app must never volunteer to be the more interesting feed. Competing for the
next hit concedes the scroll's premise in a nicer voice — and BrainDiet's whole
thesis is that a brain fed every ten seconds builds nothing. The triage "bored"
branch used to say "Then let's make it interesting"; it now says **"Boredom is
the good part."**

How to write it:
- **Normalise the feeling**, don't fix it. Boredom is what an unfed brain feels
  like, not a fault to be cleared.
- **Remove the precondition.** "Nothing has to feel interesting before you
  start" — the wait for motivation is the actual blocker, not the boredom.
- **Give doing nothing a name.** Exits say "I'll sit with it," never "Head back
  out." An endorsed choice, not a failure to convert.
- HONESTY LAW still binds: describe behaviour. Never claim boredom makes anyone
  creative, and never claim we have reset anyone's dopamine.
