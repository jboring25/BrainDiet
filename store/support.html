Common questions

What does it cost?
3 days free, then $4.99 a week, or $19.99 once for lifetime access. Lifetime never renews.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → BrainDiet. You can change or cancel there anytime.

Is BrainDiet medical or mental health advice?
No. BrainDiet helps you block distractions and build habits. Its suggestions are general ideas, not medical, mental health, or nutrition advice. If you are struggling, talk to someone you trust or a professional; in the US you can call or text 988 any time.

How do I reset my plan or my data?
Open BrainDiet → Settings, and use the reset option. Your data lives on your phone, so this clears it completely.

Does BrainDiet collect my data?
No account, no ads, no trackers. Your data lives on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is the text you write about your goals and what you did, sent to an AI model to write your steps, and never stored by us. See our Privacy Policy.

Restoring a purchase
Open BrainDiet → Settings → Restore Purchases.
