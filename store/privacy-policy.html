BrainDiet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 (rev. 2)

Short version: BrainDiet has no accounts, no trackers and no ads. Your data lives on your phone. The only things that leave it are the words you write about your goals and what you did, sent to an AI model so it can write your steps. We don't store them, and the model doesn't train on them.

Who we are

BrainDiet ("the app", "we") is an iOS app that helps you spend less time doomscrolling and more time on what matters to you. This policy explains how the app handles information.

What stays on your device

BrainDiet has no user accounts and no sign-up. Everything you create in the app (your moonshot, your milestones, your plan, your sessions, your schedules) is stored on your device using Apple's on-device storage.

What is sent to the AI, and why

To write your daily steps and your road of milestones, BrainDiet sends these to our server, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude model:

  • your moonshot and milestones, and why they matter to you, in your own words
  • the answers you gave about your day: how much time you lose to your phone, when it pulls you in, the time you can give, when you wake and sleep, and what has stopped you before
  • when you use "Add something you did", the sentence you type, so it can be counted under the right area

It never includes your name, email, contacts, location, or which apps you use. Our server does not save or log what you wrote; it passes it to Anthropic and returns the answer. Our host (Netlify) processes your IP address to deliver each request and to limit abuse. Anthropic uses the text only to return that answer, may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, and does not use it to train its models (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). If you are offline, the app builds your steps on your phone instead.

The steps BrainDiet suggests are general ideas for building habits. They are not medical, mental health, nutrition, or professional advice. If you are struggling, talk to someone you trust or a professional; in the US you can call or text 988 any time.

Screen Time and app-blocking features

BrainDiet's app-blocking uses Apple's Family Controls and Screen Time technologies. That information is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or to third parties. Apple's frameworks are designed so that even we, the developer, cannot read the specific apps or content you use.

Purchases

Purchases (the weekly subscription and the lifetime purchase) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your name, card number, or any payment details. To confirm your purchase, we use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase record from Apple. It does not receive your name, email, goals, or anything else you put into the app. RevenueCat's handling of that information is governed by the RevenueCat Privacy Policy (revenuecat.com/privacy). Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, tracking, and ads

There are none. BrainDiet contains no third-party analytics, no advertising, and no tracking software. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not build a profile of you.

Sharing your information

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information. The only third parties that ever receive anything are the ones named above (Anthropic for your steps, RevenueCat and Apple for purchases), and only for those purposes.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is stored on your device, so you are in control. You can reset it from the app's settings, and deleting the app removes all of its data. We keep no copy on our server.

Your rights, wherever you live

You can see, change, or delete everything BrainDiet holds about you inside the app, because it is all on your device; deleting the app deletes it. We keep no copy on our server, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase, but you can still email us with any request and we will answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region: the text you send to the AI is processed to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract), it is processed in the United States by the providers named above, and you can complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California, we do not sell or share your personal information.

Children

BrainDiet is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.
